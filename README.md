# My Favorite Movie: Interstellar

## Overview
**Interstellar** is a science fiction film directed by *Christopher Nolan*. It explores space travel, time dilation, and the survival of humanity.

## Plot Summary
The story follows **Cooper**, a former NASA pilot, who is recruited for a mission to find a new habitable planet as Earth faces environmental collapse.  
He travels through a wormhole near Saturn, encountering different planets where **time moves differently** due to gravity.

## Main Characters
- **Cooper** – The main protagonist and pilot  
- **Murph** – Cooper’s daughter, a brilliant scientist  
- **Brand** – A NASA scientist  
- **TARS** – A highly intelligent robot  

## Key Themes
1. Love transcending time and space  
2. Survival of humanity  
3. Relativity and time dilation  

## Favorite Quote
> "Love is the one thing that transcends time and space."

## Why I Like It
- Stunning visuals of space 🌠  
- Emotional father-daughter story  
- Mind-blowing science concepts
